## Out-of-distribution use (read before trusting a prediction)

Predictions outside this corpus rely on first-principles constants, not fitted
coefficients; measured error there is ~10.9% MAPE and unbounded beyond it.
Do not treat planner outputs as calibrated for:

- non-Selene clusters (H100, PCIe-only, TPU, or any other interconnect)
- precision other than bf16 (fp8-native training stacks, fp16, int8)
- sequence lengths other than 2048 and context-extended recipes (RoPE scaling, long-context attention variants)
- architectures that are not dense GPT-style decoders (MoE, encoders, multimodal)
- global batch shapes far outside 1,048,576 - 6,291,456 tokens

## Extending the corpus honestly

1. Add measured rows (CSV or JSON) with a citable `source_url`/DOI. The ingestors in
   `nomo_planner/simcalibration.py` (`load_evidence`, `parse_evidence_csv`,
   `parse_evidence_json`) reject rows without provenance and require ISO source dates.
2. Fit with `fit_parameters` / `fit_by_strategy` and quantify with `evaluate_held_out`
   (group-preserving train/held-out split, no leakage; see `tests/test_simcalibration.py`).
3. A new cluster needs its own measured rows before the pack reports calibrated
   accuracy; until then outputs stay on first-principles constants and are labelled
   as uncalibrated.
